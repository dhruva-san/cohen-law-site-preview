10 Reasons You Need a Commercial Real Estate Attorney for Your Next Property Deal

By Dhruva William, Marketing. Published February 16, 2025, updated May 25, 2026.

When embarking on a commercial real estate transaction, the complexities involved can be daunting. Whether you're a seasoned investor or a first-time buyer, having a commercial real estate attorney by your side can make all the difference. At Cohen Property Law, we understand the intricacies of commercial real estate, and we believe there are compelling reasons to engage a specialized attorney for your next property deal. Here are ten essential reasons why hiring a commercial real estate attorney is crucial to your success.

1. Expertise in Complex Transactions

Commercial real estate deals often involve numerous parties and intricate contracts that can be challenging to navigate. An experienced commercial real estate attorney brings the knowledge necessary to guide you through the complexities of your transaction. We understand the nuances and potential pitfalls, ensuring that you are protected at every step.

2. Contract Drafting and Review

The contracts involved in commercial real estate can be lengthy and filled with legal jargon. A skilled attorney, like those at Cohen Property Law, will draft and review contracts to ensure they align with your goals and protect your interests. We tailor contracts to your specific needs, making them clear and fair to help prevent future disputes.

3. Due Diligence Support

Due diligence is a critical aspect of any commercial real estate transaction. It involves a thorough investigation and assessment of a property to uncover any potential issues. Our commercial real estate attorneys conduct comprehensive due diligence to identify any red flags, from zoning restrictions to title issues. This process helps you make informed decisions, avoiding costly mistakes down the road.

4. Financing Assistance

Navigating the financing landscape can be overwhelming, especially in commercial real estate. Our attorneys assist you in securing the necessary funding for your project. We negotiate terms with lenders and review financing documents to ensure they are favourable to you. Having legal support during this phase can safeguard you from unfavourable terms and conditions.

5. Zoning and Land Use Compliance

Zoning laws vary significantly across regions and can influence how you can use a property. A commercial real estate attorney can help you understand these laws and ensure compliance. At Cohen Property Law, we assist clients in obtaining variances and navigating zoning restrictions, ensuring that your intended use aligns with local regulations.

6. Avoiding Common Pitfalls

Many common issues can arise during a commercial real estate transaction, such as contract disputes, title problems, or compliance issues. Our attorneys have the experience to identify and prevent these problems before they escalate. By working with us, you can avoid delays and penalties that could derail your project.

7. Negotiation Expertise

Negotiating the terms of a commercial property deal can be tricky. Our attorneys are seasoned negotiators who advocate for your best interests. We leverage our knowledge of real estate law and market conditions to help you secure favorable terms. Whether it’s lease agreements or purchase contracts, having a skilled negotiator on your side can lead to better outcomes.

8. Closing the Deal Smoothly

The closing process can involve numerous last-minute details that require careful attention. A commercial real estate attorney ensures that all necessary documents are in order and that any outstanding issues are resolved before closing. At Cohen Property Law, we handle this crucial stage meticulously, allowing you to proceed with confidence.

9. Dispute Resolution

Despite thorough preparation, disputes can still arise in commercial real estate transactions. Our attorneys are equipped to handle mediation, arbitration, or litigation if necessary. We strive to resolve disputes efficiently, protecting your interests and minimizing disruption to your business operations.

10. Tailored Legal Guidance

Every commercial real estate transaction is unique, and one-size-fits-all solutions rarely suffice. Our team at Cohen Property Law takes the time to understand your specific needs and goals. We provide tailored legal guidance that aligns with your objectives, ensuring that you receive the most relevant advice and support.
