Why You Need a Dental Attorney When Acquiring a Practice in Texas

By Dhruva William, Marketing. Published October 10, 2024, updated May 25, 2026.

Acquiring a dental practice is a significant step that requires careful planning and execution. Whether you are a recent dental school graduate or an established practitioner looking to expand your horizons, purchasing a dental practice can be a complex and multifaceted process. In Texas, navigating the legal landscape surrounding such transactions necessitates expertise in dental law and regulations. This is where a dental attorney in Texas becomes an invaluable asset.

Understanding the Legal Landscape of Dental Practice Acquisitions

Acquiring a dental practice involves more than simply agreeing on a price and signing a contract. The transaction must comply with state laws, regulations, and ethical guidelines specific to the dental profession. In Texas, the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners regulates dental practices, ensuring compliance with licensing and operational standards.

A dental attorney in Texas is well-versed in these regulations and can guide you through the legal requirements necessary to complete the acquisition. This includes ensuring that the seller is in good standing with the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners, confirming that all licenses and permits are valid, and that the practice complies with local and state health regulations.

Key Reasons to Hire a Dental Attorney

1. Navigating Complex Contracts

One of the most critical aspects of acquiring a dental practice is the negotiation and drafting of contracts. The purchase agreement outlines the terms of the sale, including the purchase price, payment structure, and any contingencies. Without a thorough understanding of the legal implications, you may inadvertently agree to unfavorable terms that could harm your practice in the long run.

A dental attorney in Texas can help draft and review the purchase agreement, ensuring that all terms are clear, fair, and legally binding. They can also negotiate on your behalf, advocating for your interests and ensuring that the contract protects you from potential liabilities.

2. Conducting Due Diligence

Before finalizing a purchase, it’s essential to conduct due diligence to assess the practice’s financial health and operational status. This process includes reviewing financial statements, patient records, employee contracts, and existing leases. A dental attorney can assist you in conducting thorough due diligence by identifying any potential legal issues or liabilities associated with the practice.

For instance, your attorney can help uncover hidden liabilities, such as outstanding debts, pending lawsuits, or regulatory violations, which could affect the value of the practice. This comprehensive review helps you make informed decisions and minimizes the risk of unexpected surprises after the acquisition.

3. Understanding Employment and HR Issues

When acquiring a dental practice, you will inherit the existing staff and employment contracts. Understanding these contracts and the associated labor laws is critical to ensuring a smooth transition. This includes compliance with Texas labor laws, employee benefits, and potential issues related to employee retention.

A dental attorney in Texas can guide you through the complexities of employment law, ensuring that all employee agreements are compliant and that you understand your responsibilities as the new owner. They can also advise on how to handle any potential employee disputes or grievances that may arise during the transition.

4. Addressing Regulatory Compliance

The dental industry is subject to various regulations and compliance standards. In Texas, dental practices must adhere to state-specific rules regarding patient privacy, billing practices, and infection control. Failing to comply with these regulations can lead to fines, legal issues, or even loss of licensure.

A dental attorney can help you navigate these regulatory requirements, ensuring that your practice adheres to all state and federal laws. They can assist in preparing the necessary documentation and guide you on how to implement compliant practices, reducing the risk of legal issues in the future.

5. Managing Licensing and Permits

Acquiring a dental practice in Texas involves more than just the transfer of ownership; it often requires updating licenses and permits to reflect the new owner. This process can be complicated, particularly if the practice holds special permits or is involved in specific healthcare programs.

A dental attorney in Texas can help manage the licensing process, ensuring that all necessary applications are submitted and that you remain compliant with the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners. They can also help you understand any changes to your professional liability insurance that may be necessary as a new owner.

6. Protecting Against Future Liabilities

One of the key roles of a dental attorney is to protect your interests and mitigate potential future liabilities. This includes understanding any claims, lawsuits, or disputes that may exist with the practice you are acquiring.

A dental attorney can provide guidance on how to structure the acquisition to limit your liability, such as negotiating indemnification clauses or advising on the formation of a new business entity to separate your personal assets from business risks. They can also help you understand how to manage and resolve any ongoing legal issues associated with the practice.

Conclusion:

Acquiring a dental practice is a significant investment that requires careful consideration and expert guidance. The complexities of the legal landscape in Texas, combined with the unique challenges of the dental industry, underscore the importance of hiring a dental attorney in Texas to assist you throughout the acquisition process.

From navigating contracts and conducting due diligence to managing employment issues and ensuring compliance with regulations, a dental attorney provides invaluable support and expertise that can help you avoid costly mistakes and ensure a smooth transition.
