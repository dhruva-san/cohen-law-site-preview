How David Cohen Law Firm Helps Dentists and Dental Specialists

By Dhruva William, Marketing. Published September 15, 2023, updated May 25, 2026.

When it comes to legal matters related to dentistry, having a knowledgeable and experienced attorney by your side is crucial. David Cohen Law Firm PLLC specializes in providing comprehensive legal services tailored to the unique needs of dentists and dental specialists. With a deep understanding of the dental industry and its intricacies, the firm offers expert guidance and representation in various practice areas. In this blog post, we will explore how David Cohen Law Firm can assist dentists and dental specialists in navigating the legal landscape and achieving their professional goals.

Dental Transitions

One of the key areas where David Cohen Law Firm excels is in dental transitions. Whether you are buying or selling a dental practice, the firm provides invaluable support throughout the process. From negotiating purchase agreements to conducting due diligence and handling the legal documentation, their team ensures a smooth transition that protects your interests and maximizes the value of the transaction.

Dental Practice Sales

Selling a dental practice requires careful planning and execution. David Cohen Law Firm has a wealth of experience in representing dentists in practice sales. They understand the complexities involved, such as valuation, buyer qualifications, and regulatory compliance. By leveraging their expertise, you can navigate the intricacies of the sales process with confidence, ensuring a successful outcome.

Dental Partnerships

Entering into a dental partnership can be a significant step in your career. David Cohen Law Firm provides strategic counsel and guidance to dentists seeking to form partnerships. They assist in drafting partnership agreements that address important aspects such as profit sharing, decision-making authority, and dispute resolution. With their support, you can establish a solid foundation for a successful and harmonious dental partnership.

Dental Business Formation and Structuring

Starting a dental practice or restructuring an existing one requires careful attention to legal and regulatory requirements. David Cohen Law Firm helps dentists navigate the complexities of business formation and structuring. They assist in selecting the appropriate legal entity, drafting operating agreements, and ensuring compliance with state and federal regulations. By partnering with the firm, you can establish a solid legal framework for your dental business.

Dental Associateship

Bringing in an associate dentist can be a significant decision for your practice. David Cohen Law Firm offers legal guidance to dentists considering associateships. They help draft comprehensive associate agreements that outline the roles, responsibilities, compensation, and termination provisions. Their expertise ensures that your associateship arrangement protects your interests and promotes a mutually beneficial working relationship.

Dental Service Organization (DSO)

As the dental industry evolves, more dentists are exploring partnership opportunities with Dental Service Organizations (DSOs). David Cohen Law Firm advises dentists considering DSO affiliations, helping them navigate the legal and contractual aspects. They review DSO agreements, negotiate terms, and ensure that dentists' rights and interests are safeguarded throughout the partnership.

Buying a Dental Practice

For dentists looking to acquire an existing dental practice, David Cohen Law Firm provides comprehensive legal support. They conduct thorough due diligence, review purchase agreements, and navigate the intricacies of financing and regulatory compliance. Their goal is to protect your investment and facilitate a successful practice acquisition.

Conclusion

Navigating the legal aspects of dentistry requires specialized knowledge and expertise. David Cohen Law Firm PLLC is dedicated to serving dentists and dental specialists with their comprehensive range of legal services. Whether you are buying or selling a practice, forming a partnership, or facing any other legal challenge, their team of experienced attorneys is ready to provide personalized and responsive legal advice. With David Cohen Law Firm by your side, you can confidently focus on your dental practice, knowing that your legal needs are in capable hands.
