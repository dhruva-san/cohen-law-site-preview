Legal Strategies to Protect Dentists' Interests in DSO Transactions

By Dhruva William, Marketing. Published August 8, 2024, updated May 25, 2026.

In the ever-evolving landscape of the dental industry, many dentists are considering entering into Dental Service Organization (DSO) transactions. These transactions can offer various benefits, such as increased efficiency, access to resources, and potential growth opportunities. However, dentists must understand the legal implications and potential risks involved in such transactions. This blog post will explore legal strategies that dentists can employ to protect their interests when engaging in DSO transactions. As a leading DSO lawyer, Cohen Law Firm PLLC is dedicated to providing dentists with comprehensive legal guidance and support throughout the transaction process.

Understanding DSO Transactions

Before delving into the legal strategies, it is essential to have a clear understanding of what DSO transactions entail. A Dental Service Organization is a business model that provides non-clinical support services to dental practices. These services can include administrative, financial, marketing, and operational support. In a DSO transaction, a dental practice may choose to partner with a DSO to benefit from these support services. However, dentists must approach these transactions with caution and ensure that their interests are protected.

1. Thorough Due Diligence

Before entering into any DSO transaction, dentists must conduct thorough due diligence. This involves carefully reviewing the DSO's financial records, operational practices, and legal agreements. Dentists should seek the assistance of a DSO lawyer, like Cohen Law Firm PLLC, to ensure that all relevant documents are thoroughly examined. By conducting comprehensive due diligence, dentists can identify any potential red flags or risks associated with the DSO and make informed decisions based on the findings.

2. Negotiating Favorable Terms

When entering into a DSO transaction, dentists should negotiate favourable terms that protect their interests. This includes negotiating the terms of the partnership agreement, financial arrangements, and operational control. Dentists should work closely with their DSO lawyer to ensure that the terms of the agreement align with their goals and objectives. By negotiating favourable terms, dentists can maintain control over their practice and safeguard their financial interests.

3. Protecting Patient Relationships

One of the primary concerns for dentists engaging in DSO transactions is the preservation of patient relationships. Dentists should ensure that the DSO agreement includes provisions that protect patient confidentiality and continuity of care. This can include clauses that restrict the DSO from soliciting patients or transferring patient information without the dentist's consent. By safeguarding patient relationships, dentists can maintain trust and loyalty within their patient base.

4. Compliance with Regulatory Requirements

Compliance with regulatory requirements is crucial in any dental practice, especially in DSO transactions. Dentists should ensure that the DSO they partner with adheres to all applicable laws and regulations, including those related to patient privacy, billing practices, and professional licensing. Dentists should work closely with their DSO lawyer to review the DSO's compliance policies and procedures. By prioritizing compliance, dentists can mitigate legal risks and protect their professional reputation.

5. Ongoing Legal Support

Even after the DSO transaction is complete, dentists should continue to seek ongoing legal support. This includes regularly reviewing the DSO agreement, monitoring compliance, and addressing any legal issues that may arise. Cohen Law Firm PLLC offers comprehensive legal support to dentists engaged in DSO transactions, ensuring that their interests are protected throughout the partnership.

Conclusion

Entering into DSO transactions can provide dentists with numerous benefits, but it is essential to approach these transactions with caution and protect their interests. By employing legal strategies such as thorough due diligence, negotiating favourable terms, protecting patient relationships, complying with regulatory requirements, and seeking ongoing legal support, dentists can mitigate risks and safeguard their professional and financial interests. As a trusted DSO lawyer, Cohen Law Firm PLLC is committed to providing dentists with the necessary legal guidance and support to navigate DSO transactions successfully.
