Who we are

Cohen Law Firm, PLLC is a multi-focused law firm serving individuals and businesses, with a unique focus on work with dentists and dental specialists including general dentists, orthodontists, periodontists, endodontists, pediatric dentists, prosthodontists and oral surgeons.

We anticipate our clients' needs. We do not merely react to them.

We are privileged to serve the legal needs of local, regional and national dentists and specialists, as well as entrepreneurs and business owners. We provide personalized services to every client and serve their diverse needs, whether in purchasing or selling their practice, bringing in an associate, becoming an associate, incorporating their business, purchasing or selling a building, or leasing a building.

We listen intently to our clients and educate them about the legal process, ensuring that every client feels like their legal matter is the most important matter at hand before us, rather than simply just another legal matter. It is our goal to ensure that our clients' needs are met from the initial consultation to completion of their matter.

It is also our goal to be conscious of clients' fees and try to contain them through a flat fee system. Because we work diligently to understand each client's business, industry and unique circumstances, we are more prepared to protect their interests. Because we are committed to frequent communication and ready accessibility, we can obtain client input quickly and move forward in an expeditious manner.
