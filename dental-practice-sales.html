Dental practice sales

Navigating the complexities of dental practice sales with expert guidance.

Request a consultation Call (972) 294-7531

The dental industry is very active with regard to buying and selling dental practices. It is becoming increasingly common for dentists to purchase existing practices and for owners of existing practices to sell and retire.

Selling a dental practice can be a complex process, but it can also be rewarding if done correctly. A dental practice transition specialist can help guide you through the process, helping to ensure that everything runs smoothly.

We here at Cohen Law Firm recognize that most dentists need help in the field of law, particularly when it comes to dental practice sales. Dentists selling their practice soon realize that it's not as easy as turning over the paperwork to the buyer. The same thing applies to the buyer side. It's not just a matter of writing a check.

Getting the protection needed

Protecting the interests of both buyers and sellers

Both parties need to be protected. Dentists selling their practices want hassle-free transitions and want to get the most out of the practice that they are selling. Buyers want to make sure that they are not buying headaches together with dental practices.

Avoiding legal disputes with smooth dental transitions

We've witnessed parties taking matters to court just because they didn't get a firm that focuses on smooth dental transition. This is what we're looking to avoid. There's a reason why you want to buy or sell a dental practice, and that's not to go to court.

Why hire the specialists?

Specialization matters

A lot of dentists are under the impression that they can just hire a law firm without taking its specializations into consideration. If you're looking to get divorced, you hire a divorce lawyer. If you're looking to sell your practice, you hire a firm that focuses on dental practice sales.

Expert guidance through dental practice transitions

Dental law firms have competent and experienced attorneys to handle the tasks involved in a practice transition, including drafting the sales agreement, reviewing documents and verifying information, and preparing necessary forms and paperwork. They are familiar with the rules, regulations and laws that apply to dental practice sales.

Finding the right buyer

Some firms also have the experience to help you find a qualified buyer. From general practice sales to orthodontic and endodontic practice sales, they can connect you with buyers interested in purchasing a practice, and help you maximize your practice's value and prepare it for the transition.

Services that you need

A law firm like Cohen Law Firm can help you in many ways when it comes to selling or buying a dental practice:

  • Independent contractor agreements (review and negotiation)
  • Writing and reviewing of sale, title and lease documentation
  • Anything related to acquisition, development and financing
  • Maintenance of the non-profit organizations of the dentist
  • Negotiating of joint venture and sale agreements
  • Incorporation of dentist entities
  • Forming of LLCs and partnerships
  • Workouts and restructurings
  • Structuring of the practice's interactions

These are just some of the services that encompass dental practice sales, but you can already see why legal representation matters for a streamlined transition.
