How to Choose an Attorney for Your Dental Transaction

By Cohen Law Firm Editorial Team. Published April 4, 2020, updated May 25, 2026.

If you are like most people, there are few things you enjoy less than going to the dentist. Working with an attorney may be one of them! Many persons have endured the misfortune of having unsatisfactory experiences with legal counsel. If this has ever happened to you, then you don’t need to be told how upsetting such an experience can be.

The good news is there are existing attorneys that create a positive experience for clients. You just need to know how to select them. For dentists, the screening process is even more important because the field is so unique. A good transactional dental attorney can help make your life easy. Let’s take a closer look at how you can make sure you wind up with the very best transactional dental attorney possible.

Experience with Dental Transactions

Choosing the right transactional dental attorney is a lot choosing any other qualified professional. You don’t want to settle for someone that isn’t familiar with dental transactions just to save a few bucks. You want to hire an attorney that has handled an abundance of dental transactions, and even better, an attorney that has worked on the exact type of situation that you are transacting. If you hire an attorney with transactional dental experience, you know you can trust them to provide you with the level of service you deserve.

Expeditiousness and Responsiveness

You’ll want to focus on finding a dental attorney with a great track record for being responsive and expeditious in their actions and communications. You should seek an attorney that will, barring unusual circumstances, respond to an email or call within the same day, complete work in an agreed upon timeline, and not be the cause for your transaction’s delay. You also should look for an attorney that will proactively keep you regularly informed on the status of your transaction.

Reasonable Fees and Transparency with Fees

You will want to find an attorney that is completely transparent regarding legal fees and who discloses such fees before any work is performed. Because the nature of transactional dental deals typically requires back and forth negotiation, it is important to see if the attorney is amenable to charging flat fees, as opposed to billing you by the hour, because hours can pile up fast. This will help contain your legal costs and should keep them reasonable.

Solid Reputation

What are previous clients, colleagues and the dental world saying about the dental attorney you’re considering? Good attorneys have a professional reputation that backs up the claims they make verbally and in their advertising. Don’t settle for someone that other people wouldn’t recommend or go to for help again if they found themselves in the same situation.

Great Customer Service

Last but definitely not least, you’ll want to choose a dental attorney that puts you instantly at ease and inspires confidence with their professionalism. You should find them easy to talk to, responsive in regards to your concerns, and communicative in general. In other words, you should simply feel good about your choice to work with them. A good dental attorney cares not only about effectuating the client’s transaction, but about the wellbeing of the client as well.
