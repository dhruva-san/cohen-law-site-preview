What criteria should be taken into account when choosing a dental lawyer?

By Dhruva William, Marketing. Published August 19, 2023, updated May 25, 2026.

Dental professionals face a wide range of legal challenges, from forming and managing a practice to negotiating contracts and complying with state and federal regulations. For this reason, it is important to have a knowledgeable and experienceddental lawyeron your side. But with so many attorneys to choose from, how can you be sure that you are hiring the right one?

Experience in Dental Law

The first thing to consider when hiring a dental lawyer is their experience in dental law. Dental law is a specialized area of law that encompasses a range of legal issues related to the dental profession. Look for an attorney who has specific experience in dental law, as this will ensure that they have a deep understanding of the unique legal challenges faced by dental professionals. An experienced dental lawyer will be able to advise you on a range of legal issues, including practice formation, buying or selling a practice, contracts, employment issues, and compliance with state and federal regulations.

Reputation and Track Record

Another important factor to consider when hiring a dental lawyer is their reputation and track record. Look for an attorney who has a strong reputation in the dental industry, as this can be a good indication of their expertise and professionalism. You can research the attorney's reputation by looking at online reviews, testimonials from previous clients, and case studies of their work. It's also a good idea to ask for referrals from other dental professionals who have worked with the attorney in the past.

Communication and Availability

When hiring a dental lawyer, it's important to consider their communication style and availability. Look for an attorney who is responsive and easy to communicate with, as this will ensure that you are able to get the answers and support you need in a timely manner. Ask the attorney about their communication policies, including how frequently they will update you on your case or legal matter, and how you can reach them in case of an emergency. It's also important to consider the attorney's availability and workload, as you want to be sure that they have the time and resources to give your case the attention it deserves.

Fees and Billing Structure

Finally, it's important to consider the attorney's fees and billing structure when hiring a dental lawyer. Look for an attorney who is transparent and upfront about their fees, and who is willing to work with you to find a fee structure that is fair and reasonable. Some attorneys may charge a flat fee for their services, while others may bill by the hour or offer a contingency fee arrangement. Be sure to ask about the attorney's fee structure and billing practices before hiring them, so you can ensure that you are comfortable with their fees and can budget accordingly.

Conclusion

Hiring the right dental lawyer is essential for protecting your practice and ensuring that you are in compliance with state and federal regulations. When looking for a dental lawyer, be sure to consider their experience in dental law, reputation and track record, communication and availability, and fees and billing structure. By taking the time to find the right attorney for your needs, you can rest assured that you are in good hands and that your legal issues are being handled with expertise and care.
