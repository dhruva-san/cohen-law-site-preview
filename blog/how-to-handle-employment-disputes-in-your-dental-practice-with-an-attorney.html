How to handle employment disputes in your dental practice with an attorney

By Dhruva William, Marketing. Published July 7, 2023, updated May 25, 2026.

As a dentist, you may encounter various employment-related disputes in your practice, including discrimination claims, wage and hour disputes, wrongful termination, harassment, and other legal matters. When these issues arise, it is essential to seek the assistance of an experienced attorney for dentists to protect your rights and interests.

Here are some tips on how to handle employment disputes in your dental practice with the help of an attorney:

Understand the Law: It is essential to have a basic understanding of the laws governing your practice and the employment relationship. This includes federal and state laws that regulate wage and hour requirements, discrimination, and harassment.

Have Clear Policies and Procedures: To avoid potential disputes, it is crucial to establish clear policies and procedures regarding employment, such as hiring practices, performance evaluations, disciplinary action, and termination procedures. This can help to minimize misunderstandings and provide clarity to your employees.

Keep Accurate Records: It is essential to maintain accurate and detailed records related to employment matters. This includes employment contracts, job descriptions, employee files, and payroll records. Keeping these records can help you defend against potential legal claims and provide evidence if a dispute arises.

Seek Legal Advice Early: If you are facing an employment dispute, it is essential to seek the advice of an experienced attorney for dentists as early as possible. This can help to protect your legal rights and minimize the potential impact on your practice. An attorney can also provide guidance on the best course of action to resolve the dispute, whether through negotiation, mediation, or litigation.

Address the Issue Promptly: Addressing an employment dispute promptly is crucial to minimize the potential impact on your practice. This includes investigating the matter thoroughly, communicating with your employees, and taking appropriate action to resolve the issue.

Mediation or Arbitration:Mediation or arbitration can be an effective way to resolve employment disputes outside of court. These processes can be less expensive and less time-consuming than litigation, and they allow the parties to reach a mutually beneficial solution.

Litigation: If mediation or arbitration is not possible or does not lead to a satisfactory resolution, litigation may be necessary. In this case, it is essential to work with an experienced attorney for dentists who can help you navigate the legal system and protect your rights in court.

In conclusion, employment disputes can have a significant impact on your dental practice. By following these tips and seeking the assistance of an experienced attorney for dentists, you can protect your legal rights and interests and minimize the potential impact of employment disputes on your practice.
